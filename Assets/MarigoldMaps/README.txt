THE MARIGOLD - maps only (models, materials, textures, colliders).
Drag Hotel_Floor3, Service_Tunnels or Night_Train into a scene. Each map starts at (0,0,0).
Materials use the Universal Render Pipeline (URP) Lit shader: use a URP project, or convert them with
Edit > Rendering > Materials > Convert... in a Built-in project.
No scripts, sounds, lights, particles or post effects are included: add your own lights.
